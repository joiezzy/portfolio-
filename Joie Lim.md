## Why Integrative Studies

SMU's Bachelor of Integrative Studies is new in Singapore. The first cohort started in August 2023, so the first graduates arrive in 2027. This is what the degree is, and the proof I have built on top of it.

- **I design my own major**

  Each student builds an individualised major around a challenge they care about, with a faculty advisor.
- **Courses from all six schools**

  I can combine modules across SMU instead of taking one pre-set combination.
- **Real work is built in**

  A credit-bearing internship and community service sit inside the curriculum.
- **Data and numeracy in the core**

  Calculus is required, with options like Computational Thinking and Spreadsheet Modelling and Analytics.
